Who we are

This policy explains how Easy Trading ("we", "us"), Tower 5, UGF–A, Sahana Apartment, Chhatarpur, New Delhi – 110074, India, handles personal data collected through easytrading.co.in. We aim to collect only what we need to respond to you, and to be clear about where it goes.

What we collect

When you use the consultation form on our contact page, we ask for:

  • your name and mobile number (required);
  • your email address and city (optional);
  • the services you are interested in, an approximate investable amount band and your preferred contact method (optional);
  • any message you choose to write, and your consent to be contacted.

If you call, WhatsApp or email us directly, we receive the details you share in that conversation, together with your phone number or email address. Please do not send account passwords, PINs, OTPs or full bank details through the website or messaging apps.

We do not run our own analytics or advertising trackers on this website at the time of writing. Like any web server, our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.

How the form sends your details

By default the form does not store your details on our web server. When you press submit, your browser composes a message and opens WhatsApp (operated by WhatsApp LLC / Meta) or your own email app, depending on the contact method you chose. Nothing is sent until you press send in that app, and from then on the message is also handled under that provider's own privacy terms.

If we later connect the form to a secure form-processing service, your details will be transmitted directly to that service for delivery to us, and this policy will be updated to name it.

How we use your data

  • to respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation;
  • to understand your goals so we can explain which approach, if any, may suit you;
  • to keep a record of our communication with you;
  • to meet legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations that apply to us.

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give when you contact us, and, where the law requires, to comply with legal obligations. We will not send you marketing messages you have not agreed to.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example email, messaging and hosting providers), with professional advisers under a duty of confidentiality, or where we are required to by law or by a regulator or court.

Third-party embeds and cookies

Some pages include content from other companies. When those pages load, your browser connects to their servers, and they may set cookies or collect technical data under their own privacy policies:

  • TradingView: live market tickers, charts and other widgets;
  • Google Maps: the office map on our contact page;
  • Google Fonts: the typefaces used on this website;
  • YouTube and Facebook: links and any embedded videos from our channels;
  • WhatsApp: when you choose to message us.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some embedded content may not display correctly if you do.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain a reasonable record of our communication, unless you become a client, in which case records are kept for the periods required by applicable law and regulation. When data is no longer needed it is deleted or anonymised.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including serving this website over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it, you have the right, subject to applicable conditions, to:

  • obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • ask us to erase data that is no longer needed, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing already carried out);
  • have a readily available means of grievance redressal; and
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us their details, please contact us and we will delete them.

Privacy requests and contact

To exercise your rights, withdraw consent or ask a question about this policy, email contact@easytrading.co.in with the subject line "Privacy request". We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
