Market risk

Investments in the securities market are subject to market risks. Please read all related documents carefully before investing.

The value of investments in equities, exchange-traded funds, derivatives and commodity-linked products can go down as well as up. Prices are affected by company results, economic data, interest rates, currency movements, government policy, global events and investor sentiment, none of which Easy Trading controls. You may get back less than you invest, and in some products you can lose more than your initial margin.

Past performance

Past performance may not guarantee future results. Any historical figures shown on this website, including year-wise returns, compounded annual growth rates (CAGR) and illustrations of how a sum might have grown, describe what happened in the past. They are not a forecast, an indication or a promise of what will happen in future. Future returns may be lower, nil or negative.

Client-reported, unaudited figures

Performance figures, client numbers, assets under advisory and retention rates shown on this website are client-reported and have not been audited or independently verified. They may not reflect the experience of any individual investor, whose results depend on the timing of investment, the strategies used, position sizes, costs, taxes and personal decisions. Comparisons with an index (such as the Nifty 50 price index) are for context only; an index does not bear costs and is not directly investable.

Futures &amp; options (F&amp;O) risk

Trading in futures and options involves substantial risk and is not suitable for every investor. Derivatives are leveraged: a small market move can cause large losses, margin requirements can rise suddenly, and positions may be closed out at a loss. Options selling can carry losses far larger than the premium received if markets move sharply.

Studies published by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) have found that a large majority of individual traders in the equity F&O segment incurred net losses. Please consider these findings, your financial position and your ability to bear losses before trading in derivatives. Margin Trading Facility (MTF) borrowing also magnifies both gains and losses and carries interest costs.
