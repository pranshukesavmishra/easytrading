    Markets fell sharply in March as COVID-19 lockdowns began, then recovered on liquidity support to end the year at new highs.

  2. 2021

    Reopening, a strong earnings rebound and heavy retail participation carried Indian equities steadily higher.

  5. 2024

    Record highs, then a correction

    Indices reached record highs in September before an autumn correction as foreign investors sold.

How the figures are computed

Methodology.

  1. 01

    Yearly returns are client-reported

    The 2020 to 2024 annual figures (50%, 39%, 35%, 38% and 41%) are reported by Easy Trading. They have not been audited by an independent third party.

  2. 02

    CAGR is compounded, not averaged

    Each year's return is applied to the previous year's ending value. CAGR is the constant annual rate that turns the starting value into the final value over five years: (product of (1 + r))1/5 − 1.

  3. 03

    Nifty 50 is the price index

    Benchmark returns use published calendar-year closing levels of the Nifty 50 price index, from 12,168.45 at the end of 2019 to 23,644.80 at the end of 2024. Dividends are excluded.

  4. 04

    Fees and taxes

    Whether the reported figures are gross or net of fees and charges is not specified. Taxes, brokerage and the timing of individual deposits and withdrawals are not reflected.

  5. 05

    Individual results differ

    Each client's return depends on when they started, the strategies used, position sizing and capital added or withdrawn. Your result may be higher or lower.
