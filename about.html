Philosophy

Three principles, in order of priority.

The order matters. Protection comes before execution, and execution comes before compounding, because a portfolio can only compound what it keeps.

  1. 01

    Capital protection

    Risk is defined first

    Before a position is taken, we decide how much it can lose and what happens if it does. Position sizing, hedges and collateral-backed margins are set at the start, not added after a bad day.

    • Maximum loss known before entry
    • Hedged or defined-risk derivative structures
    • Exposure sized to the whole portfolio
  2. 02

    Disciplined execution

    Rules over reactions

    Markets reward process more reliably than prediction. Entries, exits and adjustments follow written frameworks, so decisions look the same on a calm Monday as on a volatile expiry day.

    • Pre-planned entries, exits and adjustments
    • Data-driven analysis behind every decision
    • Regular updates so you see what was done and why
  3. 03

    Long-term compounding

    Time does the heavy lifting

    Quality equity, held with patience, is the core. Cash flow from options income and tactical opportunities supports it, but never replaces it. The goal is wealth that keeps growing across market cycles.

    • Equity held for years, not weeks
    • Income strategies that support the core
    • Periodic rebalancing back to plan
