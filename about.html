Philosophy

Three principles, in order of priority.

The order matters. Protection comes before execution, and execution comes before compounding, because a portfolio can only compound what it keeps.

  1. 01

    Capital protection

    Risk is considered first

    Before a position is taken, we aim to define how much it could lose and what happens if it does. Position sizing, hedges and collateral-backed margins are planned at the start, not added after a bad day.

    • Risk limits considered before entry
    • Derivative positions built around risk controls
    • Exposure viewed across the whole portfolio
  2. 02

    Disciplined execution

    Rules over reactions

    Markets reward process more reliably than prediction. Entries, exits and adjustments follow written frameworks, so decisions aim to look the same on a calm Monday as on a volatile expiry day.

    • Planned entries, exits and adjustments
    • Data-driven analysis behind decisions
    • Consistent updates on what was done and why
  3. 03

    Long-term compounding

    Time does the heavy lifting

    Quality equity, held with patience, is the core. Cash flow from options income and tactical opportunities supports it, but does not replace it. The aim is wealth that keeps growing across market cycles.

    • Equity held for years, not weeks
    • Income strategies that support the core
    • Rebalancing considered against the plan
