    Before you write

    A few quick answers.

    Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp. No question is too basic.

    What happens after I submit?

    Your details reach us on WhatsApp or email (whichever you choose). We will get back to you to understand your goals, your current investments and your comfort with risk, and then arrange a consultation at a time that suits you.

    What should I have ready for the consultation?

    Nothing formal. It helps to know your goals and time horizon, roughly what you already hold (shares, mutual funds, deposits or gold) and how much you might invest. The conversation is about understanding what you want your money to do and whether our approach is a good fit for you.

    Do you work with clients outside Delhi?

    Consultations do not need an office visit. We can speak over a phone call or WhatsApp wherever you are, and you are always welcome to meet us at our office in Chhatarpur, New Delhi.
